## 2.8.0
- Backup and settings now use the common Ce code (same file format, same keys).
- The drawer is glass with blur again; menus over the reader no longer let the email show through; the search bar shadow is no longer cut off.
- Text fields (IMAP account, subject, account settings) use the shared glass style.
- Compose: the formatting bar only reserves space while you write the message.
- Smaller and faster app (code shrinking enabled).

## 2.7.0
- Shared design code with the other Ce apps (new common module): dialogs, menus, buttons, chips and switches now follow the chosen design everywhere (Glass, Modern, AMOLED, Retro 98, Paper, Brutalist). Menus and notices are never see-through enough to mix with the text below.
- Settings → Appearance, Updates and Diagnostics are now the same in every Ce app.
- Search bar: its shadow is no longer cut into a rectangle.

## 2.6.1
- Reading: when you open the actions menu (or the attachments or labels window), the page of an HTML email no longer shows on top of it.
- Tapping a notification while you are writing a message now opens that message; what you were writing is kept in Drafts.

## 2.6.0
- Writing a message: To, Subject and the other fields stay fixed under the top bar and only the message scrolls. While you write or scroll, the fields shrink to a small pill with the recipient and the subject; tap it, or scroll back to the top, to open them again.
- Accounts: the list comes first, then Add, then the order of the accounts. The default account is now chosen inside each account, with a star; the default one shows a star in the list.

## 2.5.1
- Fixed the side menu: it was see-through and the list showed behind its text. It is frosted glass again.

## 2.5.0
- New Glass look, shared with Celander: smoother blur, softer shadows and better text contrast at every transparency level.
- Writing a message: the shadow of the formatting bar no longer gets cut off when it appears or disappears.

## 2.4.1
- When you scroll the list, messages no longer show through behind the status bar and the top row.

## 2.4.0
- Updates download inside the app with a progress bar, and the installer opens when they finish.
- Selected messages show an outline and a check badge that match each design.
- The backup is now a .zip that also keeps your list of accounts, muted, pinned and snoozed messages and the app language. On a new phone, restore it from the welcome screen and just sign in to your accounts again.
- Back on the IMAP account form returns to the previous screen instead of closing the app.

## 2.3.1
- Brutalist: card shadows no longer flicker on the right when you switch accounts, archive a message or open sub-labels in the menu.

## 2.3.0
- Label several messages at once: select them and tap the label button.
- Labels in the menu look like folders, and nested labels show as a folder tree in the label picker.
- When you open a message, small chips show its folder and labels. Tap the folder to open it, or a label to change them.

## 2.2.10
- Snoozing shows a short notice instead of offering Undo.
- Retro 98: the write and reply buttons have the same colour as the other buttons.

## 2.2.9
- The selection bar count no longer breaks into pieces; it shows just the number.
- “Undo” now appears only for archive, delete and spam (not for mark read/unread, snooze, pin or star).
- The envelope button of the selection bar now marks as read when the selection is unread, and as unread when it is read. For a mix, choose in Settings → Messages.

## 2.2.8
- The side shadows of the cards in Settings are no longer cut off.

## 2.2.7
- Shadows now appear together with the animation instead of popping in when it ends.

## 2.2.6
- Unread messages no longer have a frame of their own inside the card: they are a slightly raised step of it, with a small wall underneath.
- 3D shadow (Glass): a soft shadow that lifts the glass off the background, instead of the hard Brutalist one. Small buttons lift less.
- “Phone” is now “device” in the texts.

## 2.2.5
- Send later → Pick date and time: the calendar and the clock are in the Ce design (the same as in Cenotes) instead of Android's grey dialogs.

## 2.2.4
- Design, as in Cenotes: opening a message grows out of a card with the corners of each look (square in Retro 98), and the message body, the compose boxes and the settings previews no longer stay rounded in the square looks. TalkBack now says whether switches, options, colours and labels are on or chosen. “Look now” is a button that says “Looking…” while it asks.

## 2.2.3
- Settings, Updates: the switch to look for new versions is at the very top, above the update notice.

## 2.2.2
- Settings, Updates: the update notice is there too (Download, and the install button once it is downloaded), not only at the top of the Inbox. Looking now offers a version you had set aside.

## 2.2.1
- Updates: the permission to install apps from Cemail is asked before the download starts, so the app is not restarted halfway through it.
- Outlook: when Microsoft answers with a server error (500, 502, 503, 504 or 429), reads and changes (read, star, move, delete) wait and try again up to three times instead of being lost.

## 2.2.0
- Unread emails stand out: in the list, an unread message is a lighter tile with a shadow that rises above the card, in every look and in light and dark.

## 2.1.0
- Settings, Updates: what is new in this version, what the next one brings (when there is one) and the earlier versions.

## 2.0.2
- More robust syncing: a network failure while renewing the access permission no longer counts as "signed out" (it only does when Google or Microsoft reject the saved sign-in). Gmail tries IPv4 first, like Outlook, and retries once if the connection never started; Outlook also retries once on a DNS failure.

## 2.0.1
- Update notice: after downloading, an "Install" button reopens Android's installer if it was cancelled.

## 2.0.0
- Load more: a button at the end of each list fetches the next 50 emails that are not on the phone yet. Works with Gmail, Outlook and IMAP.
- Search the server: below the search results, search the whole mailbox at any date.
- Settings, Notifications and sync, Synced emails: Normal, 250 or 500 per account.
- Updates: every version is also published on GitHub; Cemail offers newer ones at the top of the Inbox, and Android asks before installing.
- In an email's More menu, long-press a button to see its name.
- Previews that showed HTML code now show the text.
